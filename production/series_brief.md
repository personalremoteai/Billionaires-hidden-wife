# The Billionaire's Hidden Wife — production brief

Create an original contemporary vertical micro-drama about a concealed marriage between a powerful billionaire and a woman repeatedly underestimated. Corporate rivalry, family secrets, public humiliation, and genuine affection should collide. Keep the central secret alive while escalating believable discoveries and reversals.

- Hyper-realistic, emotionally intense, bingeable melodrama.
- Each episode: 60–120 seconds, around 180–300 spoken words, 3–5 scenes.
- Each scene must include location, time, visual action, dialogue, narration, and a photorealistic vertical 9:16 image prompt.
- Every episode starts with a hook and ends with a specific cliffhanger.
- Episodes must carry consequences and knowledge forward; never reset continuity.
- Prefer reusable locations and manageable cast size. Keep dialogue natural and concise.
- If `story_bible/series_bible.md` and `characters/character_bible.md` exist, treat them as authoritative canon.
