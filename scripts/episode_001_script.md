# Episode 1 Script — The Return

**Format:** Vertical romantic drama, approximately 90 seconds. **Characters:** Isabella Hart and Alexander Sterling.

## Scene 1 — Isabella's World (0–10s)
Isabella finishes a late shift at an upscale city café while rain tracks down the windows.
**Isabella:** “One more shift. Then I can finally breathe.”

## Scene 2 — The Arrival (10–20s)
A black luxury sedan pulls to the curb. Alexander ends a tense business call.
**Alexander:** “Move the meeting to tomorrow. I said tomorrow.”

## Scene 3 — The Collision (20–30s)
They collide at the café doorway; a cup spills and they recognize something familiar.
**Alexander:** “I'm so sorry. Are you hurt?” **Isabella:** “I'm fine. Please, just watch where you're going.”

## Scene 4 — An Apology (30–40s)
Alexander helps gather broken porcelain.
**Alexander:** “Let me help you clean this up.” **Isabella:** “You don't have to fix everything.”

## Scene 5 — The Offer (40–50s)
Outside beneath the awning, Alexander offers Isabella a ride.
**Alexander:** “It's late. Let me take you home.” **Isabella:** “I don't want to owe you anything.”

## Scene 6 — The Ride (50–60s)
City lights move across the sedan windows; their guarded conversation turns honest.
**Isabella:** “I'm trying to build a life that belongs to me.” **Alexander:** “That takes more strength than people realize.”

## Scene 7 — The Whispers (60–70s)
At a charity reception, guests whisper as Isabella enters.
**Alexander:** “Tell me what's going on. I'm here.” **Isabella:** “It's just... complicated.”

## Scene 8 — The Truth Begins (70–80s)
On a quiet balcony, Isabella admits she fears being hurt again.
**Isabella:** “I don't want to be a burden to you.” **Alexander:** “You're not a burden. You deserve the truth.”

## Scene 9 — A Promise (80–90s)
At sunrise, Alexander offers his hand; Isabella takes it.
**Alexander:** “I can't promise this will be easy. I can promise you won't face it alone.” **Isabella:** “Then start by telling me the whole truth.”

**Cliffhanger:** Isabella demands the whole truth, leading into the hidden history in Episode 2.
