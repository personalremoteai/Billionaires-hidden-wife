# Episode 1 Narration / Voiceover Script

**Narrator direction:** Intimate cinematic voice, clear and warm, emotionally restrained. Leave short breaths between lines.

- Scene 1: “Some people learn to hide their pain behind a smile. Isabella had become an expert.”
- Scene 2: “Across the street, a man accustomed to controlling every outcome was about to lose control of one small moment.”
- Scene 3: “For one suspended second, the noise of the city disappeared.”
- Scene 4: “His apology was simple. The history between them was not.”
- Scene 5: “Trust was the one thing neither of them could afford to give away lightly.”
- Scene 6: “For the first time that night, Isabella felt seen instead of judged.”
- Scene 7: “Secrets have a way of finding the light, especially in a room full of people watching.”
- Scene 8: “Sometimes the bravest thing is not running from the person who knows where it hurts.”
- Scene 9: “Their story was far from over. This time, it would begin with the truth.”

Mix dialogue forward; keep rain and city ambience quiet. Introduce soft piano in Scene 6 and resolve gently in Scene 9.
