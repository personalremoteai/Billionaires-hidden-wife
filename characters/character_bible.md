## Reference-generation rules
- Generate one character at a time.
- Approve a canonical portrait before generating scene images.
- Save original files with stable filenames; do not overwrite approved references.
- When a face drifts, regenerate or edit against the approved reference rather than accepting the mismatch.
- Keep the visual style consistent: cinematic photorealism, natural skin texture, realistic lighting, vertical 9:16 framing.
