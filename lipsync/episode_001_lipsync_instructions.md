# Episode 1 Lip-Sync Instructions

1. Use a clean portrait/scene image with the active speaker's face large, unobstructed, well lit, and in focus.
2. Generate a separate audio clip per speaker turn; keep narration out of character lip-sync clips.
3. Send the selected image and matching audio to the configured free SadTalker Hugging Face Space.
4. For two-person dialogue, render a separate shot for each active speaker; one still cannot reliably animate two mouths at once.
5. Normalize returned video to 1080×1920, 30 fps, H.264/AAC and sync the matching audio.
6. Review mouth movement, face identity, eyes, crop, artifacts, and sync before approval.
7. If SadTalker fails, the workflow may fall back to pan/zoom. Record this honestly; pan/zoom is not lip-sync.

The shared community Space may sleep, queue, change its API, or be unavailable. Verify a real scene before scaling.
