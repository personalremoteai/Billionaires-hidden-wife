# Episode 1 — Cinematic Production Notes

**Title:** The Return — A Chance Encounter  
**Format:** Vertical 9:16, 1080×1920, 30 fps  
**Target duration:** approximately 90 seconds  
**Visual tone:** photorealistic romance drama; warm practical lights against cool rainy-city tones; natural skin texture; shallow depth of field; restrained performances.  
**Canon:** Isabella Hart and Alexander Sterling. Keep their face, hair, age, and wardrobe consistent with approved character reference images.

## Voice direction
- **Isabella Hart:** adult feminine voice, warm but guarded; tired at first, gradually vulnerable, then quietly resolute. Avoid melodrama.
- **Alexander Sterling:** adult masculine voice, low and measured; initially distracted, then sincere and emotionally restrained.
- **Narrator:** intimate cinematic trailer voice, calm and clear, with brief pauses between sentences.
- **Mix:** dialogue forward and intelligible; narration tucked slightly beneath dialogue; soft piano and restrained rain/city ambience only where available. Do not let music cover speech.

## Scene-by-scene cinematic instructions
1. **Isabella's World (0–10s):** Café after a long shift. Medium close-up and slow push-in; warm practicals, rain reflections, tired determination.
2. **The Arrival (10–20s):** Black sedan on wet street. Low tracking shot, reflections, tilt up to Alexander; city ambience and rain.
3. **The Collision (20–30s):** They collide at the doorway and recognize each other. Brief slow motion on a falling cup; cut to matched close-ups.
4. **An Apology (30–40s):** They gather broken porcelain. Focus pull from hands to eyes; intimate two-shot and restrained tension.
5. **The Offer (40–50s):** Under the awning, Alexander offers a ride. Rain bokeh, slow push-in, pauses before responses.
6. **The Ride (50–60s):** Inside the sedan, reflections drift across windows. Dashboard two-shot, alternating close-ups; subdued piano enters.
7. **The Whispers (60–70s):** Charity reception. Elegant establishing shot, blurred crowd, close-up on Isabella's discomfort.
8. **The Truth Begins (70–80s):** Quiet balcony conversation. Emotional close-ups, slow push-in, let the silence breathe.
9. **A Promise (80–90s):** Sunrise terrace. Close-up on hands, then faces; slow pull-back and fade to black.

## Render notes
Use one clean, approved scene image per scene, ideally a portrait composition with the active speaking character's face large and unobstructed for lip-sync. For scenes with two speakers, render separate talking shots or choose the active speaker as the source portrait for each dialogue clip. The supplied storyboard poster is a visual planning reference only; it is not a substitute for nine individual scene images.
