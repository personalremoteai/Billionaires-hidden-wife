# Series Bible — The Billionaire's Hidden Wife

## Core premise
Isabella Hart secretly married billionaire Alexander Sterling. Three years later, she returns with their daughter Lily, forcing the Sterling family to confront secrets, betrayal, and unresolved love.

## Format
- Planned episodes: 70
- Target episode length: 60–120 seconds (confirm before production)
- Genre: romantic melodrama / family secrets / billionaire drama
- Ending style: every episode closes on a strong reveal, reversal, or question.

## Main characters
- Isabella Hart — protagonist; resilient, guarded, emotionally complex.
- Alexander Sterling — billionaire; controlled in public, conflicted in private.
- Lily — their daughter; observant and emotionally central to the family conflict.
- Victoria Sterling — antagonist; protective of family status and willing to manipulate events.
